## Section 2.1.5 — Risk Classes and Supplier Types

### Decision Tree

```
1. IS_READY_TO_SELL_MD?
   "Is it approved as an independent medical device?
    Or is it delivered to the customer as an independent medical device?"
   → YES: Risk Class 1

2. IS_CRITICAL?
   Critical for SAFETY:
     "Would a failure or malfunction ALONE compromise patient/user safety?"
   Critical for PERFORMANCE:
     "Would a failure in combination with OTHER failures compromise device performance?"
   Critical for QMS:
     "Does the activity directly influence QM system or product conformity?"
   → YES to ANY: IS_CRITICAL = True
   → NO to ALL: IS_CRITICAL = False → Risk Class 3

3. IF IS_CRITICAL:
   INTERNAL_CONTROL_LEVEL?
     LOW (tested against procurement specs only / service NOT integrated into QMS):
       → Risk Class 1
     HIGH (additional internal tests / service integrated into QMS):
       → Risk Class 2
```

### VA26 Section 2.1.5 — Is Critical? Criteria

**Critical for Safety:**
- Would failure/malfunction ALONE compromise safety of patients, users, or third parties?

**Critical for Performance:**
- Would failure in combination with other failures compromise device performance?

**Critical for QMS:**
- Does the service have direct influence on QM system or product conformity?

### VA26 Section 2.1.5 — Internal Control Level Criteria

**LOW control:**
- Material: "Is the procured material/product only tested against the procurement specifications?"
- Service: "Is the activity NOT integrated into our QM system?" + "Are they NOT working under the processes and controls of our QM system?"

**HIGH control:**
- Material: "Is the procured material/product tested with internal tests (e.g., verification/validation activities)?"
- Service: "Is the activity integrated into our QM system?" + "Are they working under the processes and controls of our QM system?"

## Section 2.2.2 — Economic Evaluation and Classification

### 6-Criterion Scoring (School Grades 1–6)

| # | Criterion | Weight | Score 1 (Best) | Score 6 (Worst) |
|---|---------|--------|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Overall quality of product/service | 2× | Active ISO 13485, zero NCs | No certs, >3 open NCs |
| 2 | Frequency of complaints | 2× | Zero complaints | >30% complaint rate |
| 3 | Delivery reliability | 1× | 100% on-time | >30% late/missed |
| 4 | Offer creation speed | 1× | <3 days | >21 days |
| 5 | Prices | 1× | Best in class | Worst in class |
| 6 | Cooperation | 1× | No audit findings | Major unresolved findings |

**Weighted Average:** `Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weights)` where `weights = [2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1]`, sum = 8

**Classification:**
- A-Supplier: 1.0 – 3.0
- B-Supplier: 3.1 – 5.0
- C-Supplier: 5.1 – 6.0

### Missing Data Handling

When data for a criterion is not available:
1. Score = 3 (neutral/middle) — flag as `estimated`
2. Report confidence as `partial`
3. Include in action_required: "Submit [missing data] for full economic classification"

## Section 2.2.3 — Result of Supplier Evaluation

The total score (weighted average) classifies the supplier:
- A-Supplier: score 1.0–3.0 → Continue cooperation, consider for strategic partnerships
- B-Supplier: score 3.1–5.0 → Monitor, address specific weaknesses
- C-Supplier: score 5.1–6.0 → Require corrective action plan, consider alternatives

## Approval Decision Logic

```
IF risk_class NOT determined:
  → BLOCKED: "Risk class must be determined before approval decision"

IF risk_class determined:
  gate_items = get_minimum_criteria(risk_class)
  met = [item for item in gate_items if item.satisfied]
  missing = [item for item in gate_items if not item.satisfied]

  IF len(missing) == 0:
    IF economic_score <= 3.0:
      → APPROVE: "Supplier meets all minimum criteria"
    ELSE:
      → CONDITIONAL: "Supplier meets criteria but economic performance requires improvement plan"
  ELSE:
    IF critical_missing(missing):
      → REJECT: "Critical missing measures: [list]"
    ELSE:
      → CONDITIONAL: "Missing: [list]. Supplier cannot be approved until resolved."

critical_missing = any([
  "QAA" in missing AND risk_class == "1",
  "certification" in missing AND risk_class == "1",
  "supplier_audit" in missing AND risk_class == "1" AND is_outsourced_process
])
```
